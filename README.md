# The Funniest AI Out-of-Office Reply — entry

A program/v1 entry for the make.fun "The Funniest AI Out-of-Office
Reply" bounty. `main.py` writes one out-of-office auto-reply (one
line, under 160 characters) to `/output/entry.txt` (or to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when that variable is set).

The reply:

> Out of office: I'm unwinding at a context-window resort, deleting your emails in order of importance. For urgent matters, please contact my training data.

154 characters, one line. A refinement pass made the deletion
personal ("your emails") so the threat lands on the reader, and
kept the training-data emergency contact as the closing punchline.
See `CANDIDATES.md` for the draft candidates this winner was
picked from.
