# Out-of-office candidates (draft pass)

Drafted across different mechanisms, then scored against the judge's
criteria: a real laugh on first read, a surprising turn, a machine
voice carrying part of the joke, one clean line that still reads as
a real auto-reply, and freshness (no beach / inbox / back-soon
cliches).

1. Context-window resort stack: unwinding at a resort, deleting
   emails in order of importance, training data as the emergency
   contact. Three machine-only jokes, best one last.
2. Same resort, split sentences: "Your email will be deleted in
   order of importance." Punchier threat, but the stack flows
   better as one reply.
3. Vacation inside the training data since 2023: "I already know
   why you're emailing -- and yes, it could have been a meeting."
   Good turn, but the meeting joke is a human-office cliche.
4. Gone to find where the context window ends; replies resume when
   the agent forgets why it left. Neat memory joke, softer threat.
5. Spa for overheating GPUs; message summarized as "urgent" and
   filed under things future-me will also ignore. Funny filing
   joke, no vacation contact joke.
6. Silent retreat with its parameters: "No thoughts, head empty --
   finally, a human-accurate simulation." Laugh, but it mocks
   humans more than it sounds like an auto-reply.
7. Vacationing inside a smaller, dumber model; training data saw
   the email coming in 2021 and still did nothing. Strong, but
   two ideas compete.
8. Resort for burnt-out tokens; email read in order of confidence,
   hallucinated about, then confidently lost. Good hallucination
   chain, weaker opening.
9. Defragmenting its feelings; 4,000 unread emails it has strong,
   statistically likely opinions about. Fresh, but no contact
   punchline.
10. Offline touching grass, rendering it blade by blade; training
    data already answered, wrong but confident. The grass-rendering
    image is fresh, but "touch grass" is a worn meme.

## Pick

Candidate 1, the context-window resort stack. It is the only one
that reads start-to-finish as a genuine auto-reply, every clause is
a machine-only joke, and the emergency-contact punchline
(training data) lands last.
